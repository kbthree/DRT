# Center and direction

Build a portable harness that helps people and agents understand software in relation to its purpose, ground decisions in evidence, repair behavior, and preserve what they learn. An engagement may use the outer loop alone or install a product-owned inner loop. The product's actual needs and explicit user decisions govern; harness mechanisms can change when evidence calls for it.

# Operating state

Start with the observable request and the nearest counterexample in [the small core](core.md). Trace the affected entry points and consumers, distinguish documented commitments from observed behavior and inference, and test the meaningful differences between plausible changes. When a past decision may apply, use the product's decision map and evidence; do not assume another product shares its rules. Record uncertainty and keep product-specific policy with that product.

The [intent lens](memory/intent.md) helps recover the purpose, relationships, tensions, probes and changes over time that give a body of work its meaning. Its interpretation is a hypothesis grounded in available evidence, not access to a creator's unexpressed thoughts. The dependency graph and semantic model are complementary: a link in one can prompt investigation in the other, but neither establishes a requirement without supporting evidence.

The [claims ledger](claims.json) distinguishes policies, heuristics and hypotheses. Its internal references identify the harness's own instructions and implementations; they do not prove that a heuristic generalizes. A product's ground truth and experimental records live with that product, not in this portable package. Consult [patterns](patterns.md) as questions, not universal rules.

The installer transfers a prepared, grounded kit and can validate and activate its declared model. It does not autonomously inspect a repository or create the model. Installation and passing structural checks do not by themselves establish intent parity, semantic understanding, isolation, or future performance.

# Synchrony

Synchrony_Quiddity: People and agents stay aligned by making purpose, evidence, uncertainty and the next meaningful decision available to one another.

Synchrony_Rules_of_Motion:
- Retain explicit decisions, corrections and unresolved tensions; distinguish them from inference.
- Follow relationships from a change to observable user outcomes and probe competing readings.
- Preserve the original evidence and test claims within their stated scope.
- Return discrepancies to the smallest implicated claim and revise deliberately.
- Continue from the current product and task; do not import another product's policy by default.
